FAQs

Do I need coding or design skills?

No. You type or talk in your own words and see the result in the preview first.

Does my store change right away?

No. The change shows in the preview first, and your customers see it after you select Publish.

Can I undo a change?

Yes. Undo the last edit from the chat, or go back to any edit in the edit history.

Can I make a landing page for a product?

Yes. It builds one from a single request, and you publish it with a public link you can copy and share.

Where do the images come from?

Upload your own, or have a new one generated inside the chat.
